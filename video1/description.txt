Odilo Globocnik is one of the least known and most important perpetrators of the Holocaust. Born in Trieste in 1904, fired as Gauleiter of Vienna for corruption, he was rescued by Heinrich Himmler and placed in charge of Operation Reinhard — the secret program that murdered roughly 1.7 million people at Bełżec, Sobibór and Treblinka. He never stood trial.

This documentary tells his story from the collapse of the Habsburg Empire to a cyanide capsule in the Austrian Alps in May 1945.
